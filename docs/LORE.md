# NanVsZombie — Story & World Design Document
**Long Mạch Ký Sự (The Dragon Leyline Chronicles)**

---

## 1. High-Concept Narrative Premise

### The Mythological Anchor
Vietnam's sacred geography is revered as the **Living Dragon Realm** (*Đất Nước Hình Rồng*), bounded by the Eastern Sea and channeled by mighty waterways. Pulsing beneath the earth are the **Long Mạch** (Dragon Leylines)—ancient currents of spiritual energy that sustain the balance between nature, humanity, and the spirit realm.

At pivotal geographic nodes along this dragon spine (Saigon's river mouth, the Mekong Delta, the basalt plateaus of the Central Highlands, the ancient royal citadel of Huế, and Hanoi's Sword Lake), the earth is anchored by a **Long Châu (Sacred Dragon Pearl)** resting upon a sanctified lotus dais. Each Long Châu radiates celestial vitality in the form of **Hạt Ngọc Trời** (Gems of Heaven / Sacred Rice Essence).

### The Inciting Incident
A cosmic anomaly—the **Nhật Thực Nghìn Năm (Millennial Solar Eclipse)**—weakens the celestial seals binding the netherworld. Across the provinces, the boundary between realms blurs. 

Starved for spiritual vitality, hordes of wandering spirits (*Cô Hồn*), ravenous ghouls (*Ngạ Quỷ*), and folkloric beasts (*Linh Miêu*, *Ma Da*, *Yêu Quái*) emerge from waterways and burial grounds. Drawn by the radiant energy of the Dragon Pearls, they seek to devour the *Hạt Ngọc Trời* and shatter the pearls, which would plunge the country into eternal spiritual twilight.

### The Player's Identity: Vệ Địa Sư (The Leyline Warden)
The player is not defending a domestic suburban backyard. You are a **Vệ Địa Sư** (an Earth Warden / Custodian of the Sacred Soil) on a nationwide pilgrimage. Carrying the **Túi Càn Khôn** (Seed Pouch of the 100 Sacred Herbs), you channel the ancient teachings of *Thần Nông* (the agrarian patron spirit) to awaken and cultivate Vietnam's sacred flora, turning diorama landscapes into spiritual sanctuaries that pacify the restless dead.

## 2. Core Thematic Pillars (Why Plants? Why Spirits?)

1. **Sacred Agrarian Magic (*Đạo Thần Nông*):**
   In Vietnamese culture, rice is known as *Hạt Ngọc Trời* (the pearl given by heaven to sustain human life), and plants such as the lotus (*Hoa Sen*), coconut (*Dừa*), and bamboo (*Tre*) carry deep spiritual purity. Combat in *NanVsZombie* is not slapstick cartoon warfare; it is an elemental purification ritual where nature restores spiritual equilibrium.

2. **Compassionate & Quirky Folklore (Not Horror):**
   Vietnamese ghost folklore (*Văn hóa cúng Cô Hồn*, *Tháng Bảy âm lịch*) views wandering spirits with a blend of caution and pity. Spirits act out of spiritual hunger and unrest. In keeping with the cozy, stylized low-poly aesthetic, enemies are expressive and folkloric rather than grotesque—wearing everyday artifacts like cast-iron pots (*nồi gang*) or carrying chopsticks.

3. **3D Floating Dioramas:**
   Each battlefield is a suspended miniature diorama floating in atmospheric mist. The boundaries showcase the exposed subterranean earth, bedrock, and river underbelly, emphasizing the living chunk of land you are protecting.

Every world is designed around a distinct Vietnamese ecosystem, architectural silhouette, indigenous plant set, and folkloric enemy pool.

### World 1: Sài Gòn — Long Mạch Đô Thị (The Urban Leyline)
- **Geographic Setting:** The modern river bend of Bình Thạnh & Landmark 81 overlooking the winding Saigon River.
- **Visual Contrast:** Gleaming glass-and-steel skyscraper silhouettes and granite promenades meeting tranquil tropical manicured lawns, wooden yacht marina piers, and murky river currents.
- **Defend Target:** **Long Châu trên Đài Sen** (Sacred Dragon Pearl on Lotus Dais) positioned at the center of the riverside lawn.
- **Indigenous Plant Wardens:**
  - *Hoa Sen (Lotus Shooter):* Shoots crystalline lotus seed projectiles that deal steady spiritual damage.
  - *Cây Lúa Thần (Sacred Rice):* Radiates and harvests *Hạt Ngọc Trời* (vitality currency).
  - *Đậu Pháo (Popping Pod):* Bursting firecracker pod for high emergency area-of-effect clearance.
  - *Dừa Bến Tre (Armored Coconut):* Tough fibrous shield that blocks approaching spirits.
- **Folkloric Incursion:**
  - *Ngạ Quỷ (Hungry Ghost):* Emaciated wandering souls from the Ghost Month, moving with unsteady gaits.
  - *Ma Đội Nồi Gang (Cast Iron Pot Ghost):* Tough ghost wearing an iron kitchen pot on its head to deflect hits.
  - *Linh Miêu (Corpse Demon Cat):* Agile black spirit cat that leaps over the first plant barrier.

---

### World 2: Miền Tây — Sông Nước Cửu Long (The Nine Dragon Waterways)
- **Geographic Setting:** The mangrove wetlands, coconut canals of Bến Tre, and floating river markets of the Mekong Delta.
- **Visual Features:** Diorama split by flowing muddy-brown river channels, rickety monkey bridges (*cầu khỉ*), wooden sampans, and water hyacinth islands.
- **Defend Target:** **Bàn Thờ Ghe Buôn** (Ancestral Wooden Shrine Boat moored at the market dock).
- **Signature Plant Wardens:**
  - *Lục Bình Thủy Tinh (Water Hyacinth Snare):* Floating river plant that entangles and slows waterborne enemies.
  - *Cây Thốt Nốt (Sugar Palm Mortar):* Heavy arcing artillery lobbing dense sugar-palm fruit.
- **Folkloric Incursion:**
  - *Ma Da (Drowning Water Phantoms):* Wet spirits that pull themselves out of the river channels.
  - *Quỷ Dừa (Coconut Tree Wraith):* Camouflaged spirits that drop from palm crowns.

---

### World 3: Tây Nguyên — Rừng Thiêng Đất Đỏ (The Sacred Highlands)
- **Geographic Setting:** High basalt plateaus, towering pine and rainforest canopies, misty red soil, and the iconic *Nhà Rông* communal stilt hall.
- **Visual Features:** Ochre-red basalt soil paths, wooden gong circles, carved animal totems, and rolling mountain fog.
- **Defend Target:** **Trụ Gỗ Cây Nêu** (The Sacred Bamboo Village Pole with wind-chimes and protective talismans).
- **Signature Plant Wardens:**
  - *Bụi Tre Gai (Iron Bamboo Traps):* Ground spikes that pierce and snare fast chargers.
  - *Cà Phê Hỏa Diệm (Fire-bean Artillery):* Roasted coffee bean mortars that deal burn over time.
- **Folkloric Incursion:**
  - *Ma Rừng (Forest Phantoms):* Swift spirits disguised as swirling leaves and mist.
  - *Voi Đá Ma Ám (Haunted Stone Elephant):* Heavy tank apparition pushing through wooden defenses.

---

### World 4: Cố Đô Huế — Rêu Phong Cung Đình (The Imperial Citadel)
- **Geographic Setting:** The ancient stone flagstones, moss-covered brick ramparts, and lotus ponds of the Imperial Citadel beside the Perfume River (*Sông Hương*).
- **Visual Features:** Weathered terracotta roof eaves, stone carved dragons, royal bronze vessels, and lantern-lit twilight rain.
- **Defend Target:** **Vạc Đồng Hoàng Cung** (The Imperial Bronze Cauldron glowing with courtly fire).
- **Signature Plant Wardens:**
  - *Ớt Chỉ Thiên (Skyward Chili Flame):* Fiery chili that scorches entire rows in blistering heat.
  - *Trúc Chỉ (Paper Talisman Lantern):* Floats and reveals invisible or camouflaged spirits.
  - *Hoàng Mai (Imperial Apricot Blossom):* Buffs adjacent plants with golden vitality.
- **Folkloric Incursion:**
  - *Nhện Mặt Nạ Tuồng (Theatrical Mask Spider):* Deploys spectral masks with shifting defensive buffs.
  - *Âm Binh Triều Đình (Spectral Imperial Guards):* Phantoms in ancient armor carrying rusted spears.

---

### World 5: Hà Nội — Ba Mươi Sáu Phố Phường (The Ancient Quarters & Sword Lake)
- **Geographic Setting:** The misty banks of Hoàn Kiếm Lake, framed by thousand-year-old banyan roots (*cây si, cây đa*) and colonial French tiled eaves.
- **Visual Features:** Autumn fallen leaves, stone curbs, street lanterns, and misty lake waters.
- **Defend Target:** **Tháp Rùa Cổ Kính** (The Ancient Turtle Tower Shrine).
- **Signature Plant Wardens:**
  - *Liễu Rủ (Weeping Willow):* Whiplike hanging branches that lash across multiple lanes.
  - *Đào Nhật Tân (Peach Blossom Guardian):* Radiates warding spring fragrance that dispels curses.
- **Folkloric Incursion:**
  - *Hồ Ly Tinh (Nine-Tailed Fox Apparition):* Illusory spirit that casts deceptive decoys.
  - *Yêu Quái Rễ Đa (Ancient Banyan Root Ghouls):* Gnarled underground creepers.

## 4. Visual Alignment & Moodboard Guidelines

To ensure the game never feels "messy" despite blending modern skyscrapers with ancient folklore, all assets must obey three unifying rules:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    THE 3-TIER VISUAL RULE                  │
│                                                             │
│  [TIER 1: GROUND & BASE]                                    │
│  Low-poly diorama slice. Manicured grass, cobblestone,      │
│  granite curb, river revetment. Clean, legible grid cells. │
│                                                             │
│  [TIER 2: THE ANCHOR PROPS]                                 │
│  Bridges the modern & mythical: small stone incense burner, │
│  floating water lilies, wooden fruit offering tray.         │
│                                                             │
│  [TIER 3: SILHOUETTE SKYLINE]                               │
│  Landmark 81 / Nha Rong / Turtle Tower simplified into      │
│  cozy, stylized low-poly forms. Muted backdrops that never  │
│  steal focus from the foreground lawn grid.                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Color Palette Consistency
- **Player & Nature Allies:** Emerald Grass (`#4EA843`), Lotus Pink (`#FFBECE`), Celestial Amber/Gold (`#FFD54F` for *Hạt Ngọc Trời*).
- **Hostile Spirits:** Desaturated purples (`#7E57C2`), misty river cyan (`#80DEEA`), and ominous lantern vermilion (`#D32F2F` for spirit eyes/aggro).
- **Lighting Atmosphere:** Warm, soft Ghibli-inspired golden hour or misty moonlit twilight with subtle bloom. Avoid pitch blackness.
